Free content

Softfill includes free packs, and every paid pack has one drawing that is free to try. Free content has no time limit and needs no account. Each day one drawing from a paid pack also opens for free until midnight. After that it locks again; any colors you added are kept, and you can finish it once its pack is unlocked.

Packs

A pack is a one-time purchase made through the App Store. Once bought, it stays unlocked on your Apple Account and on every device signed in with it; use Restore purchases in the app on a new device. Packs are not shared through Family Sharing. Prices are shown in the App Store for your country.

Softfill Unlimited

Softfill Unlimited is an auto-renewing subscription, paid monthly or yearly through the App Store, that opens every pack in the app, including packs added later while you subscribe. It is shared with the members of your Family Sharing group.

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it turns into a paid subscription at the price shown in the App Store when you started it. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel them in your Apple Account settings; a cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Payments, refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple under its own terms; to ask for a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
